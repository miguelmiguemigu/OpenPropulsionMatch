# Propeller

Back to [[Chicken Wings]] · ranking: [[BEST]]

> [!summary] v2 result (2026-11-09)
> - **APC 12x8E** is the best prop for every competitive motor. It replaces the earlier "12x6 probably best".
> - On the 2826SL 1100 kV (Jupiter 80 A, GNB 930): 12x8E 2,537 gf static / 2,110 gf at 10 m/s, 64 A static (68 A peak); 12x6E 2,394 / 1,883 gf, 56 A static (57 A peak) ([[BEST]] run).
> - 3-blade props (11x7E-3, 12x7E-3) are off by default: the calibration comes from 2-blade bench tests.
> - Masses now from apcprop.com: 12x6E 26.9 g, 12x8E 26.1 g, 12x7E-3 39.1 g.
> - The 2024 tunnel 12x8 measured 12–21 % less thrust than the calibrated APC data. Confirm on a static thrust stand.
